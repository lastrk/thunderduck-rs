# τ Architecture Review — Synthesis

## Assessment

This codebase is architecturally healthy. Seven independent lenses looking for accidental complexity produced **zero findings that require undoing a design decision**, and the three lenses that went hunting for the obvious structural smells (16k-line analyzer, CommonOp/TypedOp duplication, per-operator resolve-then-stamp copy-paste) came back with negative results: `passthrough_schema_arm`, `scope_passthrough!`, `with_columns_plan`, and `na_fill_value_for` have already absorbed the mechanical factoring, and the two-IR duality is load-bearing (2 pre-analysis-only variants + 8 narrowed ones — Rust cannot express that in one `Op<Phase>`).

The accidental complexity is concentrated in exactly three places, all of them **migration residue rather than design**: (1) the half-finished `legacy_render` → `SelectBlock` emission migration, which still carries a catch-all dispatch, a duplicated 21-variant `unreachable!`, three Raw renderers that are plain SELECTs, and 11 child-recursion sites that route through the public `dispatch_op` entry point; (2) the error model, where `AnalyzerError::Other` (66 sites) is categorised Spark-emulated in the type and Thunderduck-boundary on the wire — the single genuinely user-visible defect in this review; (3) provably-inert scaffolding from the v1 deletion and the N9/F2 identity rollout (`StreamingConfig`, `ThunderduckError::TranspilerV2Emission`, two `#[allow(dead_code)]` anchor renderers, `EMIT_TAP`'s 135-call-site mutex, dead halves of `StructType`'s API).

Two findings describe real semantic gaps, not tidiness: `analyze_to_df` omits the `source_quals.clear()` that its `withColumnsRenamed` twin performs (τ accepts a qualified reference Spark rejects), and `ColumnReference::expr_id: Option<ExprId>` permits a state the design says cannot exist, on the identity path that `bare_dup_slot` keys off. Everything else is between 10 and 70 lines of hygiene.

Total dedupe: 34 findings → 27 entries. Four merges are noted inline (each raises confidence, since independent lenses converged).

### 1. `AnalyzerError::Other` exits as a Thunderduck-boundary error (66 sites) — CONFIRMED
